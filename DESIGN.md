# Multitasked Madness: current game design

## Continuous contribution — 31 October 2026

This specification replaces the previous worker/stakeholder/task game. Latest user agreement: four columns with identical rules initially at zero; hold one activator at a time. ## Player loop

The first press on any activator starts the 60-running-second round and immediately begins building that column. There is no separate Start control and ready time never counts. A quick press/release still starts the clock. Level 1 makes all four permanent columns available immediately; Level 2 makes only the first two available. The player holds the dedicated activator **below** a column to build that column, releases to stop, and can then hold another. Column bodies do not accept gameplay input. Accept only one simultaneous pointer/key hold; additional contacts cannot replace the active hold. Support Space/Enter on a focused activator for equivalent hold input.

Every fresh hold ramps growth linearly from zero to CONFIG.growthPerSecond (10 percentage points/s) over CONFIG.warmupSeconds (2 seconds), then maintains full speed, capped at 100%. Release, cancellation, focus loss, pause, end and retry clear the ramp. Repeated hold calls for the currently active column are idempotent; a new accepted hold after release restarts at zero. No decay applies while held, including warm-up, and contribution remains quadratic in height. An empty column fills in 11 uninterrupted seconds. Integrate ramp height and contribution analytically through the warm-up and cap boundaries. A full column does not complete, reset, earn a bonus or spawn a replacement. Holding at full keeps it full. Every unheld column decays at a constant 3.85 percentage points/second, with bounds [0, 100]. This uses the previous decay curve's midpoint and stays identical at every completion percentage and in both levels. Reaching zero is ordinary and never ends the round; empty columns can be rebuilt.

Each column contributes `(progress / 100) ** 2` internal units per second, whether held or released. Total contribution is the time integral of all four quadratic rates. Keep fractional values internally; display totals, current/average rates and percentages as integers. Multiply contribution totals, current/average rates, graph-axis labels and both live/comparison chart descriptions by CONFIG.contributionDisplayScale (100) before rounding to the nearest integer. Keep model/history values in their original units; 100% earns 100 displayed points/s and 50% earns 25. Floor height so it cannot display 100% before the actual cap. No score deductions, completion rewards, random rolls or hidden difficulty changes.

## Levels and progression

Level 1’s full 60-second timer completion unlocks Level 2, with no contribution target. Ready time, pause time and incomplete retries do not qualify. Expose a disabled Level 2 selector with an unlock hint before completion; after Level 1 results offer Continue to Level 2. Unlock is in memory for this open page and survives retries/level selection; reload resets progression. The latest completed Level 1 history is kept in memory for the Level 2 graph. No new persistence or accounts.

Level 2 has the same four physical columns and stable controls. Only the two left-hand columns are playable; the other two stay permanently grey with disabled Focus controls, zero height/contribution and no motion indicators. They can never rotate in or become active, even when the left two are full. Guard availability in both the pure model and pointer/key start path so a blocked input cannot even start the clock. The only rule difference is playable column count (CONFIG.level2Columns = 2); all other tuning is shared.

Between rounds, level selection resets the metrics/graph to ready and requires a fresh Focus press. Prevent level selection during running and paused rounds. Retry preserves the current level, while returning to Level 1 restores its four active columns. Indicate the current level in the header and name the completed level in results. Keep the current compact board and footnote.

## Time and input

The timer is the sole end condition and ends exactly at 60 running seconds. Include contribution up to that instant and nothing beyond it, even for a long frame. End freezes all state and opens a results dialog; it cannot be dismissed by Escape. Retry returns to ready, empties every column and clears time/contribution. Do not add an early quit or mid-round reset control.

Ready/paused states freeze time, height and contribution. Manual Pause and hidden tabs clear holds and require Resume. Visibility restoration alone never resumes. Visible window blur releases a hold but lets the running clock continue. Pointerup anywhere, pointercancel, current-target lostpointercapture, focused-button blur and key release clear active input. Ignore delayed capture loss from a previous control or one whose pointer has been recaptured. A fresh pointerdown with the same pointer ID recovers a missed release immediately, synchronizing elapsed time before switching; a different simultaneous pointer/key cannot steal the hold. Pointer capture lets an activator hold continue while the pointer moves outside until released. Rendered activators and their children remain connected for the entire round. Synchronize elapsed time at input transitions before changing active state.
