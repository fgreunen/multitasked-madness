# Project guidance

Read README.md, DESIGN.md and TODO.md before implementation. DESIGN.md defines the current product; TODO.md retains historical work. Keep them current with agreed changes. Latest user instructions override historical behavior.

## Current scope — 31 October 2026

A native browser game about attention and continuous contribution. Four permanent columns with identical mechanics and distinct hues start at zero. Pressing any activator starts a fixed 60-running-second round, the sole ending condition. Hold one dedicated activator below a column to raise it; the column body is not interactive. Only one simultaneous input may build. Full columns remain at 100% while held and never complete or reset.

Each fresh hold ramps growth linearly from zero to 10 percentage points/s over CONFIG.warmupSeconds (2 seconds), then stays at full speed. Release/pause/end/retry reset the ramp; repeated hold calls on the same active column preserve it. Held columns do not decay during warm-up. Integrate ramp height and scoring analytically through warm-up/cap boundaries. Show a small stable Warming up badge and progress line on the held activator until full speed; clear it whenever input clears. Released decay is a constant CONFIG.decayPerSecond (3.85) percentage points/s at every height, eventually reaching zero. Every column earns `(progress / 100) ** 2` contribution per second, held or released. Preserve fractional contribution. Keep all tuning in src/config.js and configuration-dependent labels derived from it.

No workers, stakeholders, satisfaction, importance/urgency, task arrivals/queue, sizes, scope creep, dependencies, scenarios or obsolete saved comparisons. Old session data is ignored. Do not add modes, persistence, accounts, infrastructure or settings screens without a request.

Separate rules from rendering. Integrate height and contribution by elapsed time analytically through full/zero/round boundaries. Clip at exactly 60 seconds and freeze on end. No contribution after expiry, no early game over or quit. Retry resets all state to ready. Manual Pause and hidden tabs freeze everything, clear input and require Resume. Visible blur releases input while time continues. Handle pointer capture, release outside, cancellation and current-target capture loss; ignore delayed capture loss from a previous target or recaptured pointer and recover a missed release on a new press from the same pointer ID; keep activator DOM targets connected. Support focused Space/Enter holds as an accessible input equivalent. Clear all holds across pause/resume/end/retry.

Show total contribution, current combined rate, remaining time, each column's height centered inside the top ~10% and stable Focus/Focusing activators. Omit visible column names and individual rates; explain quadratic contribution in one small footnote and a shared quarter-height-to-rate reference derived from the scoring function. Multiply displayed contribution totals, current/average rates, graph-axis values and live/comparison chart descriptions by CONFIG.contributionDisplayScale (100) before integer rounding, preserving model/history fractions in the original units. Do not scale height percentages or time. Label the combined rate "Contribution per second: +N". Results show total, average rate and the graph, without per-column statistics. Level 2 results also compare the latest completed Level 1 using point difference, percentage change, both totals and average rates, switches and average uninterrupted focus-hold duration. Count a switch on a new hold to a different column from the last focus, including across idle/pause. Average hold is total running seconds held divided by accepted fresh holds; use one decimal second. Snapshot completed Level 1 effort metrics with its history, preserve through retry/selection and clear on reload. Calculate from unrounded values; handle ties, sub-unit changes and zero baselines. Describe observed round differences without causal WIP claims. There is no audio or sound UI. Keep one short instruction, with no separate Start control or repeated instructional panels. Show a cumulative contribution-over-time graph live and in results; sample exact running seconds independently of frame size and reset/freeze it with the game. Omit up/down arrows from tracks and activators; retain accessible motion descriptions. Center desktop columns/buttons at 70% of their grid-cell width; keep small-screen controls usable. Respect reduced motion.

Level 1 has four playable columns. Completing its full round unlocks Level 2 for the open page (no score threshold or new persistence). Level 2 keeps four visible columns, but only the first CONFIG.level2Columns (2) are playable. The two right-hand columns remain permanently grey/disabled/zero and never rotate in. Block them in model and input handlers, including first-press start. All other parameters/rules stay identical. Level selection only between rounds; retry preserves level and unlock, returning to Level 1 restores four active columns. Header/results identify the level; Level 1 results offer Continue to Level 2. Reload resets progression. In Level 2 live/results graphs, show the latest completed Level 1 history as a distinct dashed comparison line, prepopulated for the entire minute with a shared scale and compact legend. Snapshot it only on Level 1 completion; retain through retries/level changes, replace on another Level 1 completion and clear on reload.
