# Multitasked Madness

## Language

**Page session**:
The rounds played while one page stays open, including the Level 2 unlock and latest completed Level 1 comparison. Reloading starts a new page session.

**Round**:
One attempt at a level, completed when its full running-time allowance expires. Ready and paused time do not count toward that allowance.

**Baseline**:
The latest completed Level 1 round used for comparison with Level 2. It remains the baseline until another Level 1 round completes or the page session ends.
